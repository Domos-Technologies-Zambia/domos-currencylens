# Privacy Policy — Domos CurrencyLens

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

Domos CurrencyLens ("the extension") is published by Domos Technologies, Lusaka, Zambia. Contact: **aaronmbuzi6@gmail.com**

## What the extension does
It finds prices on the web pages you visit and shows them converted into the currency you choose.

## Data we collect
**None.** Domos Technologies does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, browsing history or page content. There are no analytics, trackers, ads or accounts.

## How the extension works with your data
- **Page content:** prices are detected and converted locally in your browser. Page text is never sent anywhere.
- **Settings:** your chosen currency, options and the list of sites you disabled are stored using your browser's extension storage (and may sync across your devices through your browser account, according to your browser settings). We cannot see them.
- **Exchange rates:** to get current rates, the extension requests public rate data from open.er-api.com (Exchange Rate API) and, as backups, from the fawazahmed0 currency-api (via cdn.jsdelivr.net and currency-api.pages.dev). These requests contain no information about you or the pages you visit, although, as with any web request, those services can see your IP address. The rates are cached in your browser for up to 12 hours.

## Permissions
- **Access to websites (http/https):** needed to find and convert prices on the pages you view.
- **storage:** saves your settings and cached rates.
- **activeTab:** lets the popup read the current site's name so you can disable the extension on it.

## Accuracy
Rates are mid-market rates from third parties, provided for information only. The amounts you actually pay (bank, card or shop rates, fees, taxes) will differ.
